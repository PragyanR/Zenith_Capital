01Approach

How the Fund Invests

Zenith invests part of AEZ’s own funds. Members split into four pods, each running one strategy, and every position starts with research that the rest of the fund can read.

01

Fundamental Research

Stock picks start with the company: its earnings, cash flow, and what it is worth. Share price comes last.

02

Defined Risk Limits

Position sizes, sector exposure, and cash levels have hard limits. Pods work inside them, and the VP of Investments enforces them.

03

Written Pitches

Every idea goes to the committee as a written pitch that states the thesis, the valuation, and what would make it wrong.

02Portfolio

Target Allocation by Pod

Each pod has a target share of the portfolio. A pod’s share can move up to 10 points from its target, depending on how it performs.

  • Long/Short Equity 50%
  • Sector ETFs 25%
  • Fixed Income 15%
  • Derivatives 10%

Long/Short Equity

The largest pod. Members research individual companies, value them on earnings and cash flow, and pair long and short positions so the pod has little exposure to the overall market.

  • Single-name equities
  • Paired positions
  • Net beta under 0.2

25%

  • Macro research
  • Rotation reviews

Derivatives

  • Covered calls

03Risk

Risk Limits

These limits apply to every pod. The VP of Investments monitors them and is responsible for keeping the fund inside them.

Position Limit
5%
No single investment may exceed 5% of total portfolio value at entry.
Sector Cap
30%
Exposure to any one sector is capped at 30% of the portfolio.
Market Neutrality
|β| < 0.2
The Long/Short pod keeps its absolute beta below 0.2 to limit the effect of market cycles.
Liquidity Floor
10%
At least 10% of assets stay in cash or short-duration Treasuries to cover margin and withdrawals.
Allocation Drift
±10%
Each pod’s share can move up to 10 points from its target, based on how the pod performs.

04Process

From Research to Committee Vote

Every position goes through the same 5 steps.

  1. 01

    Training and Endorsement

    Brothers complete pod training and are endorsed by their Pod Leader before they work on live ideas.

  2. 02

    Research

    The pod sources an idea within its strategy, builds the model, and identifies what would prove the thesis wrong.

  3. 03

    Written Pitch

    Every recommendation is submitted as a written pitch of at least half a page, reviewed by the Pod Leader.

  4. 04

    Committee Vote

    The Investment Committee, the VP of Investments and the Alumni Advisory Council, votes on every pitch.

  5. 05

    Monitor and Report

    Approved positions are sized within the mandate, monitored by the pod, and reported in the semester investor letter.

05Structure

Who Makes the Decisions

The VP of Investments is elected by AEZ each year and appoints the Pod Leaders. The VP and the Alumni Advisory Council form the Investment Committee, which votes on every pitch.

Investment Committee

Standing body

The fund’s decision-making body. Votes on every pitch presented by the pods.

  • VP of Investments
  • Alumni Advisory Council
  • Approves all positions

VP of Investments

Annual, elected by the AEZ active body

Owns the fund’s risk management and appoints the Pod Leaders each year.

  • Enforces the risk mandate
  • Selects Pod Leaders
  • Writes the semester investor letter

Alumni Advisory Council

Alumni with investing industry experience

Brings professional judgment to the committee and gives feedback on every pitch.

  • Reviews and votes on pitches
  • Mentors pods
  • Receives investor letters

Pod Leaders

Semesterly, appointed by the VP of Investments

Run one of the four strategy pods and hold its work to the fund’s standard.

  • Train and endorse pod members
  • Ensure written pitches for every idea
  • Present to the committee

Pod Members

Active brothers of AEZ

Do the research. Any active brother can join a pod after training and endorsement.

  • Source and research ideas
  • Build models and theses
  • Monitor positions
